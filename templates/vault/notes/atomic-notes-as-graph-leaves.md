# Atomic Notes as Graph Leaves

Atomic notes are the smallest durable knowledge units in Agentic Memory. Each note should contain one reusable idea, decision, pattern, or question.

They act as leaves in the graph. Structure comes from MOCs and semantic links rather than deep folders or category tags.

Atomic notes should mature from `seedling` toward `evergreen`, with 3+ meaningful semantic links as one sign of maturity.
